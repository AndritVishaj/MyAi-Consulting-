AI Consulting

Find the one or two places AI pays off in your business, and a plan to get there.

Most AI projects stall because nobody chose a clear use case first. We start with how your business actually runs, then rank the options by effort and return so you spend only on what works.

Best when you know AI matters but not what to do first.

  • AI readiness review

    We look at your processes, data and tools to see what is ready for AI and what needs fixing first.

  • Use-case shortlist

    A ranked list of opportunities with the effort, risk and expected benefit of each, in plain language.

  • Tool selection

    Honest advice on which tools to buy, which to skip, and how to avoid being locked in.

  • Roadmap

    A phased plan with owners and timing, starting with a first win you can ship quickly.

  • Team training

    Hands-on sessions so your people can use the new tools with confidence.

AI Automation

Hand repetitive work to workflows that run on their own.

Automation follows fixed steps, so it is fast, predictable and cheap to run. We find the tasks eating your team’s week and connect the tools you already use to do them.

Best when your team repeats the same steps every day.

  • Process mapping

    We trace how work moves today and pick the tasks worth automating first.

  • Lead and customer flows

    Capture enquiries, update your CRM, send follow-ups and book meetings without manual steps.

  • Back-office workflows

    Invoices, reports, approvals and data entry moved off your people’s plates.

  • Tool connections

    CRM, email, calendar, spreadsheets and forms linked so data moves by itself.

  • Review points and monitoring

    A person checks the steps where mistakes cost money, and we watch every workflow after launch.

AI Agents

Assistants that take multi-step action, not just answer questions.

Where automation follows a script, an agent decides the next step within limits you set. That makes agents useful for work that changes from case to case.

Best when the work needs judgement, not just repetition.

  • Customer-facing agents

    Chat and support assistants that answer from your own content and hand over to a person when needed.

  • Internal agents

    Assistants for research, sales preparation, document handling and reporting.

  • Connected to your tools

    Access to your data and software, with clear permissions on what each agent may do.

  • Guardrails and logs

    Limits on actions, a record of every step, and a way for you to step in.

  • Testing and tuning

    We test against real cases before launch and keep improving the agent afterwards.

  • Everyday assets

  • Brand guidelines

    One document that keeps every future design and every new hire on brand.

The World

November 1, 2026 · By the MyAIConsulting team · Example post

This is an example post that shows how blog articles will look on this site. Replace it with your own writing.

A smaller world for every business

A small company can now answer customers, run campaigns and organise its work with the same kinds of tools as a company ten times its size. AI is lowering the cost of work that used to need a large team.

What stays human

Tools change quickly, but trust, judgement and good ideas still come from people. The businesses that benefit most pick a few clear problems, test a solution, and improve it step by step.

Where to start

Write down the tasks your team repeats every week, choose one, and try to automate it. That single result will teach you more than any long plan.
