# MyAIConsulting brand kit

**Name:** MyAIConsulting (one word, capital A and I)
**Slogan:** We help you grow with AI
**Voice:** warm, plain, human. Short sentences. No jargon. Talk like a helpful person, not a machine.

## Logo
- The mark is a sunburst ring of 64 rays. The full logo is the mark plus the wordmark "MyAIConsulting".
- `assets/brand/originals/` has the two full logos (orange on black, black on white).
- `assets/logo/` has the mark alone as SVG and PNG in black, white and orange, plus the favicon and a 512 px app icon.
- Use black on light backgrounds, white or orange on dark backgrounds.
- Keep clear space around the logo of at least a quarter of its height. Minimum size: 24 px for the mark.
- Do not stretch, rotate, outline, add effects, or recolour it outside black, white and orange.

## Colours
| Name | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Black | #0A0A0A | Dark background, logo, footer band |
| White | #FFFFFF | Light background, logo on dark |
| Orange | #FF6A1A | Buttons, highlights, rays (dark text on orange) |
| Ink | #141414 | Text on light |
| Muted | #5F5D59 | Secondary text on light |
| Line | #E6E3DE | Borders on light |
| Field | #F6F4F0 | Cards and inputs on light |
| Accent text (light) | #C2410C | Orange text on white (readable) |
| Accent text (dark) | #FF8A4D | Orange text on black |

Use orange sparingly: buttons and small accents only. Never put small orange text on white; use the accent text colour.

## Typography
- Font: **Hanken Grotesk** (Google Fonts), weights 400, 500, 600. Fallback: system sans-serif.
- Headlines: weight 500, tight letter spacing (-0.04em), large.
- Body: weight 400, 17 px, line height 1.6.

## Components
- Buttons: pill shape, orange fill, dark text. Ghost button: thin outline.
- Cards: 18 px rounded corners. Service cards use a frosted glass look.
- Both a light and a dark theme are supported.
